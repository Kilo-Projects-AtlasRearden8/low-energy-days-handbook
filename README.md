# Low-Energy Days Handbook

A kind, small-steps beginner manual for low-energy and ADHD-friendly productivity:
picking one task, gentle scheduling, rest without guilt, energy tracking, and
low-demand routines — with printable routine cards.

Made with care by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026).
Part of Asdale Dynamics' line of beginner-friendly guides and practical tools.

## What's inside

- `docs/` — The full beginner manual in plain Markdown.
  - `intro.md` — Who this is for and how to use it
  - `pick-one-task.md` — Choosing just one task
  - `gentle-scheduling.md` — Low-pressure scheduling
  - `rest-without-guilt.md` — Resting without guilt
  - `energy-tracking.md` — Tracking your energy
  - `low-demand-routines.md` — Gentle routines for hard days
  - `routine-cards.md` — How to use the printable cards
- `site/` — A static HTML version you can open in any browser, plus printable
  routine cards ready to print and cut out.
- `verify.py` — A small verification script that checks the site builds and the
  printable cards render.

## Who this is for

This handbook is for anyone who finds "productivity" overwhelming — especially
people living with low energy, chronic illness, or ADHD. The approach is kind,
small-steps, and guilt-free. You do not have to earn rest. You do not have to
be productive to be worthy.

## How to read it

Read the Markdown files in `docs/` directly, or open `site/index.html` in your
web browser for a polished, clickable version. The printable routine cards live
in `site/cards.html` — print them, cut them out, and keep them nearby.
