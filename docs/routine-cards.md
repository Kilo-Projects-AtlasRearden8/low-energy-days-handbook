# Printable Routine Cards

## What they are

The routine cards are small cards you can print, cut out, and keep nearby for
low-energy days. Each card has one gentle practice:

- Pick One Task
- Gentle Schedule
- Rest Without Guilt
- Energy Check-In
- Low-Demand Routine (blank — write your own)
- Blank card (make your own)

## How to print

1. Open `site/cards.html` in your web browser.
2. Press Ctrl+P (or File > Print).
3. Choose landscape orientation if your browser offers it.
4. Print on cardstock or regular paper.
5. Cut along the dashed lines.
6. Fold or keep flat — whatever works for you.

## How to use them

- Keep the cards where you make decisions: the kitchen table, your desk, your
  nightstand.
- On a low-energy day, pick one card and follow it.
- The blank card is yours — write a routine that fits your life.

## Design notes

The cards are designed to print cleanly in black and white. Colors are used
sparingly and disappear in grayscale printing so the cards stay readable.
