# Welcome to the Low-Energy Days Handbook

## Who this is for

This handbook is for days when everything feels too much. For days when the
dishes have been in the sink for three days, when "just do it" feels impossible,
and when the usual productivity advice sounds like it was written for a
different species.

It is especially for people living with low energy, chronic illness, burnout,
or ADHD — but anyone having a hard time is welcome here.

## The core idea

You do not have to earn rest. You do not have to be productive to be worthy.
On a low-energy day, the goal is not to "get back on track." The goal is to be
kind to yourself and do one small thing — or nothing at all.

This handbook is built around five gentle practices:

1. **Pick one task.** Just one. Not a list. One.
2. **Gentle scheduling.** Soft times, not hard deadlines.
3. **Rest without guilt.** Rest is part of the plan, not a failure.
4. **Energy tracking.** Notice your energy without judging it.
5. **Low-demand routines.** Tiny routines that ask almost nothing of you.

## How to use this handbook

- Read it when you have energy. Save it for when you don't.
- Pick one section to try. You do not have to do them all.
- Print the routine cards in `site/cards.html` and keep them nearby.
- There is no wrong way to use this. Skipping is allowed. Stopping is allowed.

## A kind reminder

If you are a healthcare worker, this handbook is for education and support —
it is not medical advice. If you are struggling, please reach out to a
trusted professional.
