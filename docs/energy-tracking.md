# Energy Tracking

## Notice without judging

Energy tracking is not about pushing yourself to do more. It is about learning
your patterns so you can work *with* your energy instead of against it.

There is no "good" energy level and no "bad" one. There is just what is true
for today.

## The practice: one word a day

Each day, pick one word for your energy. Examples:

- High
- Medium
- Low
- Empty
- Foggy
- Tired but wired

That is it. One word. You do not have to explain it. You do not have to fix it.

## Optional: note what helped or drained

If you want, add one note about what helped your energy and what took it away:

- Helped: a nap, a walk, a conversation, drinking water
- Drained: a long meeting, noise, skipping meals, worrying

Again, optional. One word is enough.

## Use it to plan gently

Over time you might notice patterns:

- "My energy is usually lowest after lunch."
- "I have more focus in the early evening."
- "Social days leave me empty the next morning."

Use those patterns to schedule gently — put demanding things when energy tends
to be higher, and protect low-energy times for rest.
