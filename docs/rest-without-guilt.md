# Rest Without Guilt

## Rest is not a reward

You do not have to earn rest by being productive first. Rest is not the thing
you do after everything is done. Rest is part of the day, just like eating or
breathing.

On low-energy days, rest is the plan.

## The practice

Give yourself permission to rest, fully and without guilt. That might mean:

- Lying down for twenty minutes
- Watching a show you love
- Staring out the window
- Saying "no" to a task today

Rest does not have to look productive. It does not have to be "self-care" in a
fancy way. It just has to be rest.

## If guilt shows up

Guilt is common. It does not mean you are doing something wrong. When guilt
arrives, try saying to yourself:

- "I am allowed to rest."
- "Rest helps me recover so I can try again tomorrow."
- "Being is enough."

You do not have to believe it all the way. Just say it.

## Rest without "doing" rest

Sometimes even "resting properly" feels like another task. If so, drop it.
There is no correct way to rest. Falling asleep on the couch is valid.
Scrolling your phone is valid. Doing nothing is valid.
