Each card has one gentle practice. Keep them where you make decisions — the kitchen table, your desk, your nightstand. On a low-energy day, pick one card and follow it.

Pick One Task

One Task Only

Just one task. Not a list. It can be tiny: drink water, hang up one towel, open the laptop. Tiny counts.

  • Choose one thing
  • Do it (or try for 5 minutes)
  • Stop — you are done
Gentle Schedule

Soft Times, Not Deadlines

Think in blocks: morning, afternoon, evening. One or two gentle intentions per block. No times attached.

  • Morning: one small thing if able
  • Afternoon: rest if needed
  • Evening: get ready for bed when ready
Rest Without Guilt

Rest Is the Plan

You do not have to earn rest. Lying down, watching a show, or doing nothing — all valid. No guilt.

  • Rest fully
  • Say: "I am allowed to rest"
  • Being is enough
Energy Tracking

Today's Energy

One word. No judging. No fixing.

  • High / Medium / Low / Empty / Foggy
  • Write the word down
  • Plan gently around it
